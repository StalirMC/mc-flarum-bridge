feat: in-game news, player reports and activity polls

- /mcbridge news (no permission needed) peeks the outbox and shows the
  latest forum announcements in game
- /report <player> <reason> submits a player report to the forum
  (POST /api/mc-bridge/report, stored in mc_reports, HMAC-signed)
- Activity polls: POST /api/mc-bridge/activity creates a poll with 2-10
  options, players vote in game with /vote <n> (POST /api/mc-bridge/vote,
  unique per player, re-voting replaces), the closed poll's results and
  winner are announced once via GET /api/mc-bridge/activity
- New mc_activities / mc_votes / mc_reports tables plus the two new
  mc_outbox columns land in an incremental migration so existing forums
  get them on the next php flarum migrate
- Docs and verification tables updated (verify.mjs now 329 checks)
